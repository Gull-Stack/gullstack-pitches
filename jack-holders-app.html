What's real and what isn't. Every figure on this page under “Where the $9,989 went” and “Your Toast invoice” is transcribed directly from your June 2026 merchant statement (MID ending 394988) and Toast invoice #INV10030049 — the line items reconcile to the penny. The modeler is exactly that: a model. Regulated debit interchange is capped at 0.05% + $0.21; card-present restaurant credit interchange is estimated at 1.60% + $0.10, plus network assessments of roughly 0.13% + $0.02 per item. Your real debit mix and your real card-tier mix will move these numbers, which is why we want the interchange detail pages before anyone signs anything. The Sales, Labor, Menu and Reviews tabs are not built — they're the roadmap, and they need a live POS feed to be real.
