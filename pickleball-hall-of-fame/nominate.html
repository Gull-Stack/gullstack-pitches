Guidelines for entry

Nominate a legend.

The Hall recognizes exceptional results on the court over a career, and exceptional contributions to the growth, development and leadership of the game.

5years

Competitors

Dominated the game for at least a five-year period. Think podium finishes at the US Open, USA Pickleball Nationals, the PPA or the APP.

8years

Contributors

Moved the sport at the national or international level for at least eight years.

The path to the Hall

Five steps. One class a year.

01

Nominate

Anyone can put a name forward. The form includes an essay of at least 300 words.

Due July 8
02

Nominating committee

Nine members vote by secret ballot in two rounds. Up to six competitors and three contributors move on.

03

Selection committee

Seven members choose up to five inductees a year: up to three players and two contributors. Each needs 75% approval.

Decided by August 15
04

The announcement

The new class is announced to the pickleball world in August.

August
05

Induction

Inductees receive their plaque, jacket and ring at the induction banquet.

Beyond the record

Character, integrity, sportsmanship and consideration for others.

Every candidate is weighed on how they played and served, not only what they won.
