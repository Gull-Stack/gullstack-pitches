Class of 2025 · Contributor

Melissa McCurley

Veteran, tournament pioneer, software entrepreneur and broadcaster. Often called the hardest working person in pickleball. Her goal was to help those who want to help pickleball grow.

Her road

From the front lines to center court.

U.S. Navy

Hospital Corpsman Second Class

Served during Operation Desert Storm, caring for service members, their families and World War II veterans.

EDS

The Navy's first common network

Led the build-out of NMCI for the Navy and Marine Corps. Set up the Navy's Crisis Response Center after 9/11, and its emergency team that restored communications after Hurricane Katrina.

EDS

Global Service Delivery Executive

Kept mission-critical data and voice running for American Express call centers, merchants and customers worldwide.

2014

PickleballTournaments.com

Took over the platform and turned it into the sport's engine for technology, operations consulting and data.

UTPR

The sport's first data-driven rating

Co-created the Universal Tournament Pickleball Rating with USA Pickleball, so ratings stayed fair as players grew almost 20-fold.

CBS Sports

A first in the booth

The first woman to broadcast pickleball on CBS Sports, and the first co-host of the syndicated Pickleball Show.

2025

Pickleball Hall of Fame

Inducted with the Class of 2025 as a Contributor.

Today

Still serving the game

Executive Vice President, Competition, for the APP Tour, and Acting President of the Pickleball Hall of Fame.

When you've been on the front lines responsible for restoring communications that saves lives, running tournament desk operations where life and death isn't on the line, it's not pressure.
Melissa McCurley, on staying calm at the biggest events
Bracketing and seeding

An online system for every format governing bodies and operators run: single and double elimination, round robin and more.

Tournament operations platform

Took events from a few hundred players to 3,000 or more, on as many as 58 courts at once.

Operations playbook

Kept the biggest tournaments running through weather, power and internet failures, and moved match calls to text.

UTPR

With USA Pickleball, the first algorithmic, data-driven rating system in the sport.
