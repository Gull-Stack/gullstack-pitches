Four small things, found by looking closely

What a growing
store collects.

01
Subscriptions are partway through a move.New plans on your product pages run on Skio. Recharge’s cart pieces still load, and “Manage Subscriptions” still opens Recharge’s portal. That in-between stage is when subscriber questions pile up.
02
Two free-shipping lists that don’t match.The cart names 8 exclusions and the FAQ names 11. The Everyday Bowl is on the FAQ’s list and not the cart’s.
03
Two processing times.The FAQ says 2–7 business days. The shipping policy says 2–8.
04
Replies take 1–3 business days.That’s fine for a question about aprons. A meat box that arrived thawed is a same-day problem.

Everything above is on your public site as of 14 September 2026. Detail and sources on the analysis page.

What we build and run

Four systems
under your new hire.

01
The Morning Report

Every number the posting names, on one screen by 6 am. Sales, conversion, order value, ad return, subscriptions, support, and what sold out overnight.

02
Support drafts

The common tickets get a reply drafted from your own policies: where’s my order, thawed box, delivered but missing, skip a delivery. A person on your team reads it and sends it.

03
Launch checks

Before a drop goes live: price, subscription plan, stock, shipping rules, and whether every list on the site says the same thing.

04
Subscription care

Finish the Skio move cleanly. Every Recharge subscriber accounted for, and a note from your team to anyone who cancels.
