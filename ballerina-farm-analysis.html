02The subscription move

Three things on the live site show a move from Recharge to Skio in progress. None of this is a problem on its own. The risk sits in the gap, while subscribers can still land in either system.

  • New plans run on Skio. The subscription options on the Pumpkin Pie Farmer Protein page come from Skio.
  • The subscriber portal is still Recharge’s. “Manage Subscriptions” in the footer points to /tools/recurring/get-subscription-access, Recharge’s portal path.
  • The cart still loads Recharge’s upsell, and a script named SkioHidePlansInRechargeUpsell hides 15 Skio plans inside it.

What we can’t see: how many subscribers are on each system, and when the move is meant to finish.

04Support, from the customer’s side

  • Reply window: “1-3 business days (M-F)”, from the contact page.
  • Thawed meat: the shipping policy promises a replacement box, given a name and order number.
  • Delivered but not received: a four-step self-check, then contact within 7 days.
  • Lost in transit: no tracking update for 7–10+ business days.
  • Returns: none on food; non-food within 21 days, customer pays return shipping.
  • Old help links: /pages/faq, /pages/help, /pages/shipping and /pages/subscriptions all redirect to the homepage, not to the FAQ.

Every item above has a written answer already. That’s why drafted replies can quote your policy instead of inventing one.

07What we did not measure

  • Sales, conversion rate, average order value. These need Shopify access.
  • Ad return by channel. This needs Northbeam.
  • Subscriber counts and cancellations. These need Skio and Recharge.
  • Ticket volume and reasons. These need the support inbox.
  • Site speed and mobile experience. We didn’t test them, so we make no claim.
  • What checkout charges for shipping. We didn’t place an order.
