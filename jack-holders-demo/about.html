Good Questions

Before you come in.

Do you serve breakfast all day?

Yes. Every breakfast item — omelette soufflés, benedicts, pancakes, crepes, the whole list — is available from the moment we open at 7am until we close. You can order a rib eye and eggs at 8pm.

What are your hours?

We're open 7:00am to 9:00pm Monday through Saturday, and 7:00am to 8:00pm on Sunday. Seven days a week.

Can I order online for pickup?

Yes — order online any time we're open and it's usually ready in about 20 minutes. You can also call the restaurant directly at (408) 613-2365.

Do you take reservations or large parties?

We're a walk-in dining room, but we're glad to plan ahead for larger groups, birthdays and team dinners. Call us at (408) 613-2365 and we'll get the table set.

Do you sell gift cards?

We do. Gift cards are available online in any amount and are delivered by email, so you can buy one five minutes before you need it.

Is there parking, and are kids welcome?

There's a free lot right out front on Meridian. We're a family-friendly tavern — high chairs, a kids-welcome dining room and a patio that works for strollers and dogs alike.

Do you have vegetarian and lighter options?

Plenty. The Healthy Choice breakfast section runs egg-white scrambles and a keto scramble, and lunch has a black bean burger, the vegetarian sandwich, flatbreads and six farm-fresh salads.

Hungry now?

Start an order for pickup, or just walk in — we're open until 9.
