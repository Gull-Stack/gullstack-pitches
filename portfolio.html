01 · The first thirty days

Measure first. Build only what the baseline says to.

The same sequence at every company, so every later number has something to be compared against. Each example below is a real company we worked on this year.

01

Week one is the baseline, written down

Search rankings, traffic, analytics, every form and phone link, and where each lead actually lands. At one fiduciary wealth firm the baseline was 31 keywords, 58 visits a month, and 96.55% of that from the firm's own name. For anyone who did not already know the name, it did not exist. We reported that before we quoted anything.

Source: Semrush, live domain, 31 Jul 2026
02

The baseline decides what gets built first

That same firm had one page already earning: a 401(k) benchmarking calculator holding six commercial keywords at $3.85 to $9.95 CPC. Not an article. A tool. So the tool got built first, and the homepage waited.

Source: Semrush keyword table for the page
03

Sometimes the problem is plumbing, not marketing

A Utah drinkware brand on a modern headless storefront had every one of its 489 product pages pointing Google at a checkout domain that pointed back, so none of them could rank. No structured data anywhere. Its product customizer ranked for nothing while "custom water bottles" draws 8,100 searches a month. We rebuilt the customizer and sent them the finding free, with no strings.

Source: crawl of the live store, 2 Sep 2026
04

The site and the ads ship together, into the company's own accounts

A Utah home builder: site live on 2 September, three Google Search campaigns live on 17 September, one landing page per campaign, a 66-term shared negative list, and every lead posting into the builder's own CRM. Ad spend runs on the builder's card, not ours. We manage it.

Source: the Google Ads account and the CRM intake, 17 Sep 2026
05

When search collapses, we find out why before anyone spends

A Utah instrument maker went from 2,751 backlinks to 814,939 in one month, all spam, and its ranking keywords fell from 525 to 72 by July. The incumbent agency's proposal called it "814.9K link equity retained." We read the link data, dated the attack to a five-day window in May, and wrote the recovery plan around removing it.

Source: Semrush backlink history, Apr to Jul 2026

03 · How it works

Flat, per company, month to month.

01

Priced per company, flat and monthly

No percentage of revenue. No long contract. Each company is its own line, so a partner can add one or stop one without touching the others.

The build is included

When the baseline says a site should be rebuilt, the rebuild is part of the monthly fee. There is no separate build invoice. A site we built is a site we can do great work on.

Google, Meta, and the analytics all live in accounts the company owns. If we ever part ways, nothing has to be migrated and no data leaves with us.
