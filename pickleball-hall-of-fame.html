Our mission

The Pickleball Hall of Fame memorializes the *Champions and *Contributors of the past while creating a connection to present and future players. We preserve the history of the game while reinforcing the value of pickleball as a *sport *for *all.

Every candidate has shown character, integrity, sportsmanship and consideration for others, and earned the respect of their peers.
