What conexed.com already has

  • Google already knows the brand. Google’s AI describes ConexED and cites conexed.com directly. Once the facts on the page are right, the answer it gives is right.
  • Every AI crawler gets the full page. GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot all receive HTTP 200. Nothing is blocked.
  • Clean technical scores. Lighthouse on the phone homepage: SEO 100, Best Practices 100, Accessibility 97. At phone width, the homepage and /demo fit the screen.
  • Independent sites already list ConexED. G2, Capterra, GetApp, TrustRadius, Washington’s SBCTC, the Zoom Marketplace, LinkedIn, Facebook and a Google business listing.
  • Nothing here asks for a redesign. Every fix sits underneath the pages ConexED already has, or adds new pages beside them.

Where the opportunity is

  • Google’s AI already quotes conexed.com, so the numbers it quotes matter. Asked “What is ConexED and how many colleges use it?”, Google AI Mode answers “100+ colleges, universities, and state-wide systems” and “10 million+ students,” citing the homepage. Those figures come from a homepage block that is hidden from visitors, and /about and /demo give different counts. One confirmed number for each fixes it at the source.
  • A few crawl basics aren’t in place yet. robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt return the 404 page, no page declares a canonical, and www.conexed.com serves the same site as conexed.com. Google currently shows the www address in branded results.
  • Some old-site addresses return 404. Nine of them (/our-story, /about-us, /careers, /customers, two cohort pages and three /post/ articles) are still in a search index. A redirect for each passes their history on to the current pages.
  • There is no structured data yet. No page carries schema.org markup and there is no brand-facts.json, so machines have no structured version of ConexED’s facts to read.
  • Two pages send 0 in place of their stats. The homepage results block and /solutions both send “0%” in the HTML. The numbers count up in the browser, and crawlers read the starting value.
  • Buyers ask questions no page answers yet. There is no comparison, category or question page. For “alternatives to EAB Navigate360,” Google cites Civitas Learning’s own comparison page.

One deploy fixes it for people and for machines

  • Render the real numbers in the HTML the server sends, let the animation play over values that are already correct, and show the results section again. ConexED’s strongest proof comes back for visitors in the same step.
  • Once each count is confirmed, the same value goes into the visible page, the structured data, brand-facts.json and llms.txt. An AI engine trusts a fact it finds stated the same way in four places.
  • These numbers are ConexED’s to confirm. Each one is a question below.

Method and limits

  • The crawl: all 15 reachable URLs on conexed.com, fetched on 24 November 2026 between 09:20 and 09:50 MT as Googlebot, every figure taken from the HTML the server sent, and rendered in Chrome at desktop and phone widths.
  • The AI answers: Google AI Mode, signed out, US English, the morning of 24 November.
  • Lighthouse: the phone homepage scores SEO 100. Lighthouse marks robots.txt and canonical checks as not applicable when they are absent, and leaves structured data to a manual check, so the two views fit together.
  • Not measured: Search Console, Semrush, backlinks, ChatGPT answers and field Core Web Vitals. Nothing missing has been estimated.
